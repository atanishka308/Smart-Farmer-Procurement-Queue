# Stakeholders

## Primary Users
- Farmers bringing rice or wheat to procurement centres

## Key Stakeholders
- Procurement-centre staff
- Procurement-centre operators
- Agriculture/procurement officials
- Relevant procurement agencies

## Roles

| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| Farmers | Bring produce and wait for procurement |
| Centre staff | Manage procurement and queue |
| Officials | Support procurement operations |
| Procurement agencies | Manage procurement systems and records |

## Access Plan

- Interview 4+ farmers
- Interview 1–2 centre staff
- Observe the procurement process with permission
