# Farmer Evidence

## E01 — Farmer Interviews

**Participants:** 3 farmers

### Key Findings
- Farmers bring rice/wheat to the procurement centre.
- Produce may be procured the next day or later depending on the queue.
- Farmers are uncertain about when their produce will be called.
- Waiting time and schedule uncertainty are major concerns.

### Evidence Status
Reported by farmers — E01

### To Validate
- Actual waiting time
- Queue position/status visibility
- How farmers currently receive schedule updates
