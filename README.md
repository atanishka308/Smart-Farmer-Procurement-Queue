# Smart Farmer Procurement Queue

**Team:** 5 STACK
**Team ID:** P-2025-29-CD-013-AY26-27
**Vertical:** Cloud & DevOps (CD)
**Mentor:** Ramachandran Gururaj

## Problem Statement

Farmers bringing rice or wheat to government procurement centres face uncertainty and delays because procurement schedules, queue status, and procurement/payment information are not always clear or timely.

## Key Issues

* Long waiting times at procurement centres
* Uncertainty about when produce will be procured
* Limited schedule and queue-status information
* Repeated enquiries and follow-ups
* Travel and time burden for farmers

## Target Users

**Primary:** Farmers
**Key Stakeholders:** Procurement-centre staff and officials

## Current Situation

Farmers bring their produce to the procurement centre and wait according to the queue. Their produce may be procured the next day or even the following week, making it difficult to know when they will be called.

## Evidence

**E01:** Interviews conducted with 3 farmers.

## Key Validation Focus

* Actual waiting time
* Schedule and status information availability
* Farmer information needs
* Centre-side queue and data availability
