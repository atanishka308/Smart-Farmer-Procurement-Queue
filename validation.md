# Validation Plan

## What We Need to Validate

- Actual farmer waiting time
- Time taken from arrival to procurement
- How farmers receive procurement schedules
- How farmers check queue/status
- Number of repeated enquiries or visits

## People to Engage

- 4+ farmers
- 1–2 procurement-centre staff

## Evidence to Collect

- Farmer interviews
- Direct workflow observation
- Waiting-time measurements
- Centre-side process information

## Privacy

- Get permission before interviews, observation or photos
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information
- Keep farmer details private
