# Farmer Interviews

## E01 — Farmer Interviews

**Participants:** 3 farmers

### Key Findings

* Farmers bring their rice or wheat to the procurement centre.
* Produce may be procured the next day or even the following week, depending on the queue.
* Farmers are uncertain about when their produce will be called for procurement.
* Long waiting times and lack of clear schedule/status information create uncertainty.
