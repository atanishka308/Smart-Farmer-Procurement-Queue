# Procurement Centre Observations

## Observation Record

### What We Observed

* Farmers bring rice or wheat to the procurement centre.
* Produce is handled according to the existing queue/process.
* Farmers may need to wait before their produce is procured.
* Information about when a farmer's produce will be called may not be immediately clear.
