# Current Procurement Workflow

1. Farmer harvests eligible rice/wheat
2. Farmer takes the produce to the procurement centre
3. Produce is placed in the queue
4. Farmer waits for their turn
5. Procurement takes place according to the queue
6. Farmer checks procurement/payment status

## Main Pain Points

- Long and unpredictable waiting
- Uncertainty about when the produce will be procured
- Limited schedule and queue-status information
- Repeated enquiries with centre staff
